Method — this is ground truth, so it must not be influenced by any model.
  1. Write what is said, not what would be correct. Keep errors, hesitations and readbacks.
  2. Numbers as spoken: « flight level three five zero », not « FL350 ».
  3. Mark what you cannot make out as [unclear] rather than guessing.
  4. If French and English alternate in one transmission, tick « Both ».
  5. Stuck? Type what you can, then « Show model readings ». Order matters: your blind attempt is kept, and the clip is marked assisted so that assisted and unassisted clips can be scored apart. The readings are unlabelled and shuffled — you cannot favour a model you cannot identify.
