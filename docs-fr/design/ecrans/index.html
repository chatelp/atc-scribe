Clairance · final screens

Every screen is assembled from the design system only: its tokens.css and components/bundle.css, no style of its own. Open one to see it at full size. They are the reference for the migration of the interface.

A · Right nowThe default: the thread, the sky, the context column on Right now.A · TrafficThe flat list and its filters; the map shows only what the filters keep.A · A frequencyThe frequency menu: volume, only this frequency in the thread; "3 new exchanges" after scrolling up.B · Column foldedThe thread widens into a transcript; Right now becomes a card over the map.C · Following a flightIts story in the column, its track on the map, its approach in the PAR frame.C · Its detailsThe Details tab: phases, instructions heard, basic info, folded sections.A · Layers, an airportThe map controls (base map, layers, opacity) and an airport's card.A · Event logThe bell: emergencies, go-arounds, runway changes, aircraft of interest, the station; its sounds.The PAR viewThe existing PAR, with its header and arrival sequence, in the sky's place.SettingsA page over the screen: Listening, Callsign matching, Display, Account, System.Sign inThe mark, one sentence, two fields.First runWho may open Clairance: this machine, or the network with a password.StatesNothing on air, connection lost, click to resume listening.PhoneOne pane at a time: Radio, Sky, Traffic, Right now; touch sizes.
