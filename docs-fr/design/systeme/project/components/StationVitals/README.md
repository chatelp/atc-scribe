# StationVitals

The station's vital signs: a few aggregated figures in a console, shown on the sign-in page before signing in, to say the station is alive and what it does.

**Markup.** `cl-vitals` › `cl-vitals__head` ("This station, live" · "refreshed every 10 s") and `<dl class="cl-vitals__list">`: `<dt>` a lowercase key in `color-text-tertiary`, `<dd>` the figures in mono, a `cl-vitals__ok` dot when the part is working (the shape of `StatusLight` ok; a failing part shows the light's ■ and its word instead), separators in `<span>`.

**What it may show** (validated 13/10): aircraft tracked, ADS-B messages per second, the farthest aircraft's distance; channels on air and transmissions today; the transcription model, its device and its time per transmission; the share of transmissions matched today; airports followed and runways in use; uptime, version, commit. **Never** a position, a callsign, a named frequency or anything that locates the station.

**Data.** `GET /api/v1/vitals` (13/10): public, frozen by a 10 s server cache, turned off by Settings › Account (`vitals.enabled`; the route then answers 404 and the console is not drawn). Keys: `aircraft`, `messages_per_second`, `farthest_nm`, `channels_on_air`, `transmissions_today`, `matched_today`, `matched_share` (integer %), `transcription_model` (a readable family), `transcription_backend` ("Apple M2 Pro", or "mlx"), `seconds_per_transmission` (one decimal), `airports_followed`, `runways_in_use` (each runway of every followed airport's active set once, whatever the direction: De Gaulle facing east = 4), `uptime_seconds`, `version`, `commit`. **A key that is absent is unknown: drop that figure, and the whole line if nothing is left on it; never write 0 for it.** Built from: counts from the live state; transmissions and matched share from one query on the day's database; the farthest distance computed from the station's configured position (a distance locates nothing); the model as a readable family ("Whisper large-v3 ATCO2", from the sidecar's `/health`); the device from a `backend` field to add there; the mean time per transmission from a rolling average to add on the Go side; version and commit set at build time. A test checks that no position, callsign or named frequency leaves the route. A setting in Settings › Account turns the console off.
