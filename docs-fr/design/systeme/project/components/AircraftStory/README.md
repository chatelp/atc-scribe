# AircraftStory

The aircraft story is what the context column shows while a flight is followed: who it is, where it is, what happened to it.

**Markup.** A `‹ Back to Traffic` header (`cl-tabs--column` height); `cl-story__head` (`PhaseBadge`, `cl-story__callsign`, operator, type and registration in `cl-plain`, a `cl-story__grid` of `cl-label` / `cl-story__val` pairs); then the tabs Story · Details · Track · Nearby (`cl-tabs--inset`). Story is `<ol class="cl-story__events">`: `<time>`, a dot (`is-radio` for what was heard, `is-now`) and a sentence; an estimate is `is-next`. Details is `KeyValues` behind `Disclosure`s, with the phase history and the instructions heard as tables (the "Then" column from `www/story/then.js`, card K, fed by each instruction's `value` and `unit`: FL, ft, deg, kt, MHz, hPa, code, runway); Track is the mini-map and its table; Nearby the proximity list.

**Rules.** The column's content is replaced, not overlaid, at the column's own width (380 px: the map does not move when a flight is chosen); ‹ returns to the tab it came from and ✕ on the callsign chip after the brand, in the top bar, stops following. The story is the **current flight**, from the aircraft's last appearance after a gap; the day's earlier flights fold under one `Disclosure` ("Earlier today: 2 flights"), in Story and in the phase history alike. A radio event is one line ("Handed to Approche De Gaulle", or "Controller on 124.350: Descend to 3 000 ft", the instruction chips' words); a click scrolls the thread to the exchange, where the transcript lives. The first line says where it was seen: "Seen by the receiver, FL 210, 61 NM north-east". An airport is named with a phase only when it is plausible (in an arrival sequence, or under FL 100 within 20 NM); otherwise the phase alone. The map note says what the map shows: "TVF414K · its track since 14:28Z", not "Following" (the map is centred once, it does not follow). Each line says a fact and its source: "Seen by the receiver", "Handed to Approche De Gaulle", "Expected touchdown, from its distance and speed".

**Then.** What the aircraft did after an instruction, from its track, in one of sixteen forms and never a verdict (card K, `www/story/then.js`). `L` is a level as the brand writes it ("4 000 ft" under the transition altitude, "FL 110" above), `D` a delay (", 2 min later", ", 40 s later").
- Level: "level at L, D" (reached and held 30 s) · "at L, D" (already there, or no movement) · "climbing through L, D" / "descending through L, D" · "climbing through L" (still on its way when the window ends) · "on the ground, D".
- Heading: "turned to 270°" · "turning through 270°, D" · "heading 254°, still turning" · "heading 254°, D".
- Speed, always named as ground speed, since the instruction gives an airspeed and the wind sits between them: "ground speed 225 kt, D" · "slowing through 250 kt ground speed, D" · "slowing, 241 kt ground speed".
- Frequency: "next heard on 119.255, D" · "next heard, D" · "no later transmission heard here".
- Runway and clearance: "landed on 27R" · "took off from 09".
- Nothing to say (no value, no track point in the window, a QNH, a squawk, a report rather than a request): "—".

Never "deviation", "complied", "error", "violation", "wrong", nor "still" except in "still turning"; no colour, no ranking.

**Data.** The aircraft's `created_at` and first track point; matched transmissions whose `plain` has a frequency role (hand-offs); `store.arrivals` (touchdown estimate).

Under the head's grid, a row `cl-row` with the label "Show only" (`cl-label`) and two `FilterToggle`s (`cl-toggle`): **"FL 340 ± 500 ft"** (the aircraft's level when pressed, in feet on the QNH under the transition altitude as the slider writes it: "2 400 ft ± 500 ft") and **"APP"** (its phase code, the one its label shows, exact: APP is not ARR). A toggle sets the Traffic filter it names: the altitude slider to that level ± 500 ft, the phase filters to that code alone; both can be on together; pressed again, it puts back what the filter was before. `is-on` as every filter toggle. An explicit row rather than a clickable level or badge: it says what it does, it is reached the same way on the phone (44 px there) and with the keyboard, and it needs no hidden gesture. The followed aircraft stays on the map and in the list whatever the filters, even once its own phase or level has moved on.
