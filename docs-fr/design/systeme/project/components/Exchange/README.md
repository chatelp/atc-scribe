# Exchange

An exchange is one transmission in the thread: who spoke to whom, what was said, what it means.

**Markup.** `<article class="cl-exchange">`: `cl-exchange__head` (time · frequency in `cl-exchange__when`, the speaker chip, →, the addressee's callsign chip, `MatchStrength`, the play button `cl-exchange__play`), `cl-exchange__text` (the transcript, `lang="fr"` when French; the words that named the aircraft in `<b>`, a search hit in `<mark>`), `cl-exchange__instr` (instruction chips), `cl-exchange__explain` (the plain-language explanation; `--boxed` when it is a sentence), `cl-exchange__reply` ("↩ Pilot replied, 4 s later", optionally the reply in a `<q>`).

**Variants.** `is-selected` (the exchange of the followed or selected aircraft), `is-unmatched` (no aircraft named: the chip says why), `is-urgent` (MAYDAY, 7700), `is-live` (someone is speaking: `LevelMeter` only). `cl-exchange--wide` is the transcript layout of the folded column: `cl-exchange__who` (time, frequency) left, `cl-exchange__what` right.

**Rules.** A transmission whose match is not decided yet shows "being matched…" in `color-text-tertiary` where the chips go, for the few seconds it takes, and "not matched" if no answer came within two minutes (the server stopped meanwhile); unmatched, the line says why in words, never in the matcher's terms: nothing named, "no aircraft in range was named"; a best candidate under the floor, its callsign chip `is-away` ("ASL86T?") and "too uncertain to name", the score left to the help bubble; two candidates too close, "could be AFR12 or AFR21". "Pilot replied" needs the server to link an instruction to its reply: until it does, the line is not shown. State what happened, never whether it was right: "Pilot replied", "No reply heard: the pilot may be out of the station's reach". A guess says it is one. The thread grows at the bottom; a new exchange appears, it does not slide. At the bottom, the thread stays at the bottom whatever changes size (an exchange that gets its chips, an old one dropped at the top); only the reader's own scroll takes it away from there, and then new exchanges gather in `NewBelow`.

**Data.** `/api/v1/transcriptions` (`speaker_type`, `callsign`, `match_score`, `match_reason`, `plain[]` with `text` and `text_fr`), `store.plainFrench(t)`, `/transcriptions/{id}/audio`.
