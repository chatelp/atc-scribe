# SettingsPage

The settings page opens over the screen: a navigation on the left, sections of fields on the right.

**Markup.** `cl-page` (on `color-scrim`) › `cl-page__inner` › `cl-page__nav` (links, `aria-current` on the section in view) and `cl-page__body`: `cl-page__head` (`cl-page__title`, a `StatusLight` "Saved", "Reload from the server", close), then `cl-page__section`s with an `<h3>` and `cl-field`s. A field is `cl-field__label`, its control (`Switch`, `Checkbox`, `cl-select`, `SegmentedControl`, `SearchField`) and an optional `cl-field__help` sentence.

**Sections.** Listening (on air, reference airport, also follow), Callsign matching (three presets; the eleven rules folded behind a `Disclosure`), Display (this browser: speech bubbles, smoothing, times in UTC or local), Account, System (log level, database size, the read-only reports, folded).

**Phone.** The nav becomes a scrolling row above the body. A field whose value is a sentence (On air) takes `cl-field--text` and goes under its label; a switch stays on the label's line.

**Names from the AIP** (Q55, 15/10), in Callsign matching, after the eleven rules:
- a field "Names from the AIP" with a `StatusLight` and its help, which says what the list is and what it is not, in words a listener reads: "The official names around the station, from the French eAIP: reporting points, control positions, procedures. They do not decide which aircraft is speaking: tried, a callsign whose airline is misheard sounds just like a procedure." (and, once the thread shows them, "They name the procedures and points heard in the thread."). No ticket number (Q55) and no "matcher" in the page: those are words of the project, not of the listener. The light in five states: ● "Up to date · AIRAC 2026-10-01"; ▲ "AIRAC 2026-10-29 in force; this list is of 2026-10-01"; – (`--off`) "Not made yet: the matcher works without it"; ◐ (`--switching`) "Updating from the SIA…"; ■ "Last update failed at 14:20Z: <why> (the list of 2026-10-01 stays in use)";
- `KeyValues`, read only: reporting points, control positions, procedures (counts), the area ("120 NM around the station") and the aerodromes ("LFPG · LFPO · LFPZ (the ones followed)");
- a `cl-row`: the button "Update from the SIA", `cl-btn--primary` when the list is out of date or absent, plain `cl-btn` when it is up to date, disabled while updating; beside it, `cl-plain` "Five public pages of the eAIP, about 9 MB, a few seconds.";
- the source line the SIA asks to cite, always shown when there is a list, `cl-plain`: "Source: SIA (DGAC), eAIP France, AIRAC cycle of 2026-10-01: ENR 4.4, ENR 2.1, AD 2 LFPG, LFPO, LFPZ. Made on 15/10 at 14:20Z. Next cycle: 2026-11-26.";
- a field with a `Switch`, "Update at each new AIRAC cycle", **off by default** (a request to an outside site: the server asks nothing of the SIA until the button is pressed or this is turned on), help "Every 28 days, when a new cycle comes into force, the server downloads the pages again."

**Naming the procedures in the thread** (Q55 usage 3, 16/10), in "Names from the AIP", under the source line:
- a `Switch` "Name the procedures heard in the thread", **on by default** (measured: at 0.95, 18 names on the owner's 77 cases, all right), help "A dashed chip beside the words gives the official name they probably were: "ELCOB 7G" for "elcope seven golf". The transcript is left as heard.";
- under it, a `SegmentedControl` of three settings, only measured ones, no free slider (a score between them would promise what nobody measured): **Surer** (1.10), **Measured** (0.95, the default), **More** (0.90); a `cl-plain` line says what the chosen one gave on the owner's listening: "18 names, all right" · "9 names, all right" · "19 names, 18 right". The field's help adds: "They name the procedures heard in the thread, from the sound of the words."

**What depends on where the station is** (owner, 16/10: the tool will serve other stations than Paris).
- **Every value that was measured or decided for a place says so**, on a `cl-field__source` line under its help, as the SIA's source line does: "Measured on Paris approaches (CDG, ORY, LBG), October 2026." (the matching presets), "Measured on the owner's listening of Paris approaches, 77 cases, October 2026." (the procedure names' setting), "Set for the Paris area: set your own for another station." (the frequency sectors, radii and ceilings), "Useful where French is spoken on the radio." (the French second reading). No decision number in the page.
- **A section "Regional data"**, after Callsign matching, holds the regional *sources*: "Names from the AIP" moves there (the matcher does not read it: it is a source for the thread, not a matching rule), with the naming of procedures and its setting. Measured values stay with their function and carry their source line: a setting is looked for by what it does, not by where it was measured.
- **A source that does not cover the station says so, without controls**: the light in `--off` and the reason, "Not for this station: the French eAIP covers aerodromes in France (LF…); this station follows CYYZ." No button, no switch. When it covers part of the followed aerodromes, the source line ends with "Not read: EGLL, outside the French eAIP." (from the API's `coverage` and its list of aerodromes outside it).
